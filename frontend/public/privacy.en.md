# Privacy Policy

**DaonGil** (hereinafter referred to as "Service") values users' personal information and complies with the "Personal Information Protection Act".

### 1. Personal Information Items Collected
The Service collects the following personal information for membership registration, smooth customer consultation, and provision of various services.
- **Required Items**: Google account information (email, name, profile picture), nickname, couple connection code, service usage records
- **Optional Items**: Date of birth, gender, anniversary information, location information (when registering a place), photos and memory data

### 2. Purpose of Collection and Use of Personal Information
The Service uses the collected personal information for the following purposes.
- **Service Provision**: Couple connection, diary and location sharing, content provision
- **Member Management**: Identity verification, prevention of unauthorized use, handling of complaints
- **New Service Development and Marketing**: Development of new services, provision of event and advertising information

### 3. Retention and Use Period of Personal Information
In principle, after the purpose of collecting and using personal information is achieved, the information is destroyed without delay. However, if it is necessary to preserve it under relevant laws, it will be stored for a certain period.

### 4. Destruction Procedures and Methods of Personal Information
- **Procedure**: Information entered by the user is transferred to a separate DB (or separate document box in case of paper) after the purpose is achieved and stored for a certain period according to internal policies and other relevant laws before being destroyed.
- **Method**: Personal information stored in electronic file format is deleted using a technical method that cannot reproduce the record.

### 5. Rights of Users and Legal Representatives and How to Exercise Them
Users can view or modify their registered personal information at any time and may request cancellation of subscription.
